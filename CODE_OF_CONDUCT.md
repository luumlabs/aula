# Code of Conduct

Aula is made for students everywhere, and we want its community to feel the same: welcoming, patient and kind. This
code applies to everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, reviews, comments and any other space of the
project.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in Aula a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## What we expect

- Being kind and respectful, including when we disagree.
- Remembering that many people here are students, beginners or writing in a second language: explain, don't mock.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Aula and for the community as a whole.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information — such as a physical or email address, or anything from someone's courses —
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities of the maintainers

The maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and take fair corrective action in response to any behavior they
find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that don't follow this code, and will explain their reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies within all of the project's spaces, and also when someone officially represents the project in
public spaces.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, tell the maintainers privately:

- on GitHub, open the **⋯** menu of the comment, issue or pull request → **Report content** → *Report to repository
  admins*; or
- for anything that shouldn't be written on a public thread, use the private form at
  [**Security → Report a vulnerability**](https://github.com/luumlabs/aula/security/advisories/new) and start the
  title with «Conduct:». It reaches only the maintainers.

Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers respect the privacy and security of
whoever reports an incident.

## Enforcement

The maintainers follow these guidelines to decide the consequences of any action they consider a violation:

1. **Correction** — for inappropriate language or other unprofessional or unwelcome behavior: a private, written
   warning that explains the problem. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — for a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued
   behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with the maintainers, for a
   specified time.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior: a temporary ban from any
   kind of interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement
   of groups of people: a permanent ban from any kind of public interaction within the community.
